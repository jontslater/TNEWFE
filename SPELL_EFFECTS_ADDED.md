## 🎯 Why Spell Effects Are Perfect

### Advantages:
- ✅ **More Visible** - Effects show during combat (fireballs, shockwaves)
- ✅ **Easier to Implement** - Sprites don't have targetable gear
- ✅ **Better Visual Impact** - Players see effects when heroes fight
- ✅ **Combat-Focused** - Effects appear during attacks/abilities

### Implementation Ideas:
- Special fireball effects (for casters)
- Special shockwave effects (for melee)
- Lightning/ice/fire variations
- Unique particle effects per tier
