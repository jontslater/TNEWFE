# TheNeverEndingWar — Monetization Strategy & Roadmap
### Version 1.0 — Prepared for Early Development & Funding

## 1. Core Monetization Philosophy

### 1.1 Guiding Principles
TheNeverEndingWar will follow a strict **no pay-to-win** monetization philosophy to preserve fairness, viewer enjoyment, and long-term trust.

**Players may NEVER purchase:**
- Power upgrades  
- Stat boosts  
- Exclusive weapons/gear with combat advantages  
- Faster XP/loot progression exclusive to paying users  

**Players MAY purchase:**
- Cosmetics (skins, VFX, nameplates, trails, pets)  
- Community-wide buffs that help *everyone equally*  
- Vanity enhancements  
- Season pass cosmetic tracks  
- Founders/Supporter prestige items  

---

## 2. Monetization Overview (High-Level)  

The game’s revenue model consists of **four phases**:

1. **Phase 1 – Early Revenue (Week 1–2)**  
2. **Phase 2 – Lightweight Cosmetics & Token Shop (Week 2–4)**  
3. **Phase 3 – Founders Pack & Art Reinforcement (Month 2)**  
4. **Phase 4 – Scalable Cosmetics + Season Pass (Month 3+)**

---

## 3. Phase 1 – Early Monetization (Immediate)

### 3.1 Supporter Vanity Perks
- Special name colors  
- Supporter badge  
- Supporter-only chat emotes  
- Access to a “Supporter Hall”  
- Discord role  

### 3.2 Bits/Donation Combat Triggers
- **100 Bits – Battle Horn** (+10% XP to all)  
- **500 Bits – Blessing Wave** (mass heal/shield)  
- **1000 Bits – Meteor Strike** (big AoE damage)

### 3.3 Minimal Cosmetic Store
- Sprite color variants  
- Weapon aura glows  
- Spell color variants  
- Nameplates  
- Trail VFX  

### 3.4 Early Token Shop
Premium currency bundles ($3, $5, $10, $20).

---

## 4. Phase 2 – Basic Cosmetic Ecosystem

### 4.1 Categories
- Hero skins  
- Weapon/Spell VFX  
- Nameplates  
- Pets  

### 4.2 Cosmetic Data Structure
- `OwnedCosmetics`  
- `ActiveCosmetics` per hero  

### 4.3 Cosmetic Crates
Randomized purely cosmetic loot.

---

## 5. Phase 3 – Founder Pack & Artist Investment

### 5.1 Founder Pack
Price: **$15–$30**

Includes:
- Exclusive title  
- Unique frame  
- Exclusive aura  
- Premium currency  
- Founder statue  
- Discord role  
- Early art previews  

### 5.2 Invest in Art
Commission:
- 3–5 skins  
- 2–3 VFX  
- 1–2 pets  

### 5.3 Public Announcement
Let players know Founder Pack funds go to artists.

---

## 6. Phase 4 – Mature Monetization

### 6.1 Season Pass
Free & Paid tracks, cosmetic only.

### 6.2 Guild Cosmetics
- Guild banners  
- Guild hall decorations  
- Guild aura  
- Guild pet  

### 6.3 Community War Funding
Server-wide goals unlocked with donations.
